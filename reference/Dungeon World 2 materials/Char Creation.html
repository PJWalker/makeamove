The foundation of a PC is their Class, the fantasy archetype they embody and express. A PC's Class influences their specialties and fortes. There can be only one of each Class at the table at the same time so, if multiple people want to play the same Class, work with each other to find a happy compromise. Briefly describe your character, using their name, pronouns, and Class, as well as incorporating details such as their Origins and Equipment/Appearance choices. If this character is joining a group in the middle of an ongoing campaign, then the talk will primarily focus on this new PC.

2) Consider Your Origins8) Create Relationships
11
These are three aspects of your character that have no specific mechanics associated with them, but that establish important fictional positioning about them. They are your character’s Ancestry, Community, and Background. Every PC has a Relationship with another PC, such as a belief, event, goal, or feeling related to them. Each Class lists some examples. The Depth of a new Relationship starts at 0, but increase it by 1 if you’ve adventured together before, and increase it by another 1 if you grew up together. (For multi-session adventures or campaigns, you can create one Relationship with each other PC now or add them when you Comfort or Support someone).

5) Assign StatsWhen your character has changed significantly due to choice, consequence, or circumstance, it may be time to change their Class. Changing a character's Class typically represents a new chapter in their life. They may not have forgotten the past and how they used to do things, but they’ve left a significant part of that behind. You can change Classes when you Level Up.
25
There are five Stats in Dungeon World 2. Your Class will guide you on how to assign them:.
◆ Strength (STR) represents how good you are fighting, pushing others around, and enduring harm
◆ Dexterity (DEX) represents how good you are at disappearing, moving quickly, aiming precisely, and escaping notice
◆ Intelligence (INT) represents how much you can notice, study, deduce, and calculate
◆ Wisdom (WIS) represents your ability to empathize with people and intuitively understand the supernatural
◆ Charisma (CHA) represents your ability to sway people, get their attention, and make a good impression
