1. Lines & Veils (From Consent in Gaming Forms)2. X-Card

Based upon the information you sent the GM through the Consent in Gaming forms, they compiled these lines and veils, which outline themes and topics we want to avoid or have only in the background.

Lines are hard limits. These are things that are not allowed to be brought into the game, even as references. If there is a line for something, it doesn't exist in the game world.

Veils are boundaries. We can introduce these, but avoid describing them explicitly. They should mostly stay in the background, and we should fade to black or cut away when they come up during play.

Anyone can add a line or veil at any time, but they should let the the group know when one gets added.		The X-Card is an optional tool (created by John Stavropoulos) that allows anyone in your game (including you) to edit out any content anyone is uncomfortable with as you play. Since most RPGs are improvisational and we won't know what will happen till it happens, it's possible the game will go in a direction people don't want. An X-Card is a simple tool to fix problems as they arise.

Whenever something comes up in play that makes you uncomfortable outside of the fiction, please let us all know by sending an "X" in the chat or by sending the GM a private message if you wish to remain anonymous.
